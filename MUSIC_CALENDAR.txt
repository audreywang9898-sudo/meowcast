TAIPEI MEOWCAST — MUSIC & CALENDAR MAP
=======================================
Purpose: Reference list for automatic Taipei Meowcast BGM selection.
Priority: Special Holiday > Solar Term > Everyday Music

A. SPECIAL HOLIDAYS
-------------------
01/01  New Year's Day       -> New Year Cat
02/14  Valentine's Day      -> Valentine Love Cat
10/31  Halloween            -> Halloween Cat
12/24  Christmas Eve        -> Christmas Eve Cat
12/25  Christmas Day        -> Christmas Cat
12/31  New Year's Eve       -> New Year's Eve Cat

Notes:
- Holiday tracks are original procedural Meowcast themes.
- Christmas tracks use an original festive/Christmas mood; they do not copy an existing song.
- Valentine's Day uses a slower, warm and romantic mood.

C. EVERYDAY MUSIC
-----------------
If today is neither a special holiday nor a solar term, Meowcast uses the
weekday-based everyday BGM library:

Monday    -> Fresh Start
Tuesday   -> Sunny Steps
Wednesday -> Cozy City
Thursday  -> Little Adventure
Friday    -> Happy Paws
Saturday  -> Slow Morning
Sunday    -> Soft Sunshine

D. CURRENT SELECTION RULE
-------------------------
1. Check whether today is a configured special holiday.
2. If yes, use that holiday's BGM.
3. Otherwise, check whether today is one of the 24 solar terms.
4. If yes, use that solar-term BGM.
5. Otherwise, use the normal weekday BGM.

Future candidates:
- Lunar New Year / 春節
- Lantern Festival / 元宵節
- Dragon Boat Festival / 端午節
- Mid-Autumn Festival / 中秋節
- Other Taipei/Taiwan-specific commemorative or seasonal days
